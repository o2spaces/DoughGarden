# DoughGarden V24 — Add-on Preset Research Notes

Date: 2026-09-09

## Key evidence used

1. King Arthur Baking — Baker's Math
https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2022/04/28/what-is-bakers-math-and-how-can-i-use-it-in-my-everyday-bread-baking
- Nuts, olives, and dried figs: 20–30% is a useful starting point.
- Denser breads can tolerate additions up to ~40%; open-crumb breads can lose structure when overloaded.

2. King Arthur Baking — Don't be a bread hostage
https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2020/04/06/dont-be-a-bread-hostage
- A 20% total-additions starting point is explicitly suggested for seeds, toasted nuts, dried fruit, olives, and herbs.
- Dry additions such as seeds, cracked/flaked grains, and toasted nuts can be soaked in an equal weight of warm water.

3. The Perfect Loaf — Walnut Cranberry Sourdough
https://www.theperfectloaf.com/walnut-cranberry-sourdough/
- Formula: walnuts 20%, sweetened dried cranberries 14%, walnut oil 3%, hydration 85%, salt 1.9%.
- This is used directly for the Walnut + Cranberry preset.

4. The Perfect Loaf — Dark Chocolate-Cherry Sourdough Bread
https://www.theperfectloaf.com/dark-chocolate-cherry-sourdough-bread/
- Formula: dark chocolate chunks/chips 20%, dried cherries 20%, cocoa 3%, oil 3%, sugar 2%, salt 1.8%, hydration 75%.
- The recipe explicitly says dried cherries can be replaced by dried cranberries.
- This supports the DoughGarden Dark Chocolate + Cranberry preset.

5. King Arthur Baking — Chocolate Chip Sourdough Bread
https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/chocolate-chip-sourdough-bread-recipe
- Example: 85g semisweet chocolate chips with 200g bread flour = 42.5% chips in a soft sandwich loaf.
- DoughGarden uses a more moderate 30% as an easier starting preset for artisan-style doughs.

6. Breadtopia — Corn Cheddar Jalapeño Sourdough Breads
https://breadtopia.com/corn-cheddar-jalapeno-sourdough-breads/
- Example: 115g cheddar with 300g wheat flour and 20g jalapeño, about 38% cheese and 7% jalapeño by flour weight.
- DoughGarden starts lower at 25% cheddar + 5% jalapeño to leave more structural margin.

7. King Arthur Baking — Sourdough Rye with Walnuts
https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/pro/formulas/sourdough-rye-with-walnuts
- Walnut inclusion is 25% in a professional rye formula.

8. King Arthur Baking — Nutty-Fruity Sourdough
https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/nutty-fruity-sourdough-recipe
- A dense sourdough with dried fruit and nuts is a documented pairing; the recipe uses 184g fruit + 113g nuts with 300g AP + 78g rye flour before preferment accounting.

## Design rule

Preset percentages are starting formulas, not hard limits. The app should keep all add-ons editable because dough strength, flour type, crumb target, fruit moisture, and cheese type change the practical sweet spot.
