# Starter Hydration Update

## V28 · BREAD STYLE BUILDER
- Starter hydration is selectable from 40% through 100%.
- Recipe math splits starter mass into flour and water using the selected starter hydration.
- Saved recipes preserve starter hydration; older recipes default to 100%.
